How the vote works

  • Communities submit fighters built on one of the five fighter templates. Staff review each one; approved fighters go on the next ballot.
  • Voting runs for the last two weeks of a season. Each eligible account gets 3 votes, one per fighter, and can take them back until voting closes.
  • To vote, an account needs a verified email, to be at least 14 days old, and 20 bets placed.
  • Counts stay hidden until voting closes. The top two join the roster when the next season starts, with a debut tournament and First Editions in the shop.

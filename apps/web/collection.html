How cards are collected

  • A fighter's card is yours once you've watched it fight on the watch page, or bet on a fight it was in.
  • Win a bet on a fighter and its card is Backed, with how many times you've called it right.
  • The collection is just for you: nothing is bought, and nothing is lost by missing a day.

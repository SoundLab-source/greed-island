How to play

Greed Island is a fighting-game stream that never stops. The fighters are computer players; you bet on them with Salt, free play money, and you can own fighters yourself.

1. Watch and bet

  • Every fight has a betting window of about a minute. Pick Red or Blue and a stake. You can change your bet until betting closes; only your last bet counts.
  • The odds come from the fighters' ratings, so an underdog pays more. They lock when betting closes.
  • Fights are best of three rounds. A draw, a crash or a timeout is a no contest: every bet is refunded.

2. Salt

  • You start with 400 Salt. Claim more every day, and if you run out, take a bailout so you're never locked out.
  • Salt is free play money. It can never be bought, sold, deposited, withdrawn or cashed out, and it has no value outside the game.
  • Tournaments use T-Salt: everyone gets 1,000 for each tournament, kept apart from Salt. It never turns into Salt.

3. Own fighters

  • The shop offers six fighters at a time and changes every few hours. The first copies of each fighter are numbered First Editions.
  • Your fighter joins the stream in tier P and climbs by winning. Everyone bets on its fights, and you earn Salt each time it wins.
  • On My fighters, spend Salt on stat upgrades (they're capped, so nobody can buy guaranteed wins), pick a sidegrade, choose the title, name plate and badges shown on stream, and ask for a custom name.
  • Challenge other players' fighters to exhibition matches.

4. Fighters and tiers

  • Every fighter is built on one of five templates: the Brawler (all-rounder), Striker (rushdown), Bruiser (heavy), Wrestler (grappler, with throws) and Sage (zoner, with a projectile).
  • Ratings decide the tier: P, B, A and S, plus X for the very best. Most fights are between fighters of the same tier, so they're close.
  • Records, ratings and titles stay with a fighter for good.

5. Seasons, titles and the vote

  • Seasons last 8 weeks. The leaderboard is Salt won this season, so it starts over each season. Balances and fighters never reset.
  • At a season's end, the best fighter becomes Season Champion and the best bettor Season Top Bettor.
  • Communities submit new fighters. In the last two weeks of a season, players vote, and the top two join the roster next season.

6. Keep your account

You start playing straight away, anonymously, in this browser. Add your email on the account page to keep your Salt and fighters on any device; we send a sign-in link, so there's no password.

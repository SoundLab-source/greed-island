Shop

Six fighters at a time, a new selection every few hours. A fighter you buy is yours: it fights on the stream, everyone bets on it, and you earn Salt when it wins.

How owning a fighter works

  • New fighters start in tier P and climb by winning. Ratings, records and titles stay with the fighter for good.
  • The first copies of every fighter are numbered First Editions.
  • Grow your fighters with stat upgrades and give them a look on My fighters.
  • Salt is free play money: it can't be bought, sold or cashed out.

Privacy

Draft for legal review. Not final.

What we keep

  • Playing anonymously: a random account with your Salt, bets and fighters. Your browser keeps a session key so you stay signed in.
  • With an email: your email address, used only to send sign-in links and to contact you about your account or a fighter you submitted.
  • Your activity in the game: bets, purchases, upgrades, votes and the names you choose. Leaderboards and fighter profiles show your player name, never your email.
  • Linked wallets: the public address you prove you own by signing a free message, and which NFTs from approved collections it holds.
  • Submitted art: the images you upload for a fighter, visible to you and staff until a fighter is approved.
  • Server logs: technical records such as IP addresses, kept for a short time to run and protect the service.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • We don't take payments, so we hold no payment details.

Others involved

  • An email provider delivers sign-in links.
  • The video and chat on the home page are Twitch embeds; Twitch's own privacy policy applies to them.
  • NFT holdings are read from a Solana data provider using your public wallet address.

Your choices

You can stop playing at any time. To have your account and email deleted, contact us from the email address on the account.
